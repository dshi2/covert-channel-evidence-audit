# Design figure caption

## Report 2 — F2

File: `report2_evidence_audit_design.png` (also PDF and SVG).

**Suggested caption.** Evaluation of evidence sufficiency in a synthetic covert-price-signalling scenario. Each of 20 blocks contains five programmed mechanism types, each presented under four evidence views in separate auditor calls (400 planned cases per model). Public and decoder views are supplemented by partial or complete policy-trial records at a fixed recipient state. The partial view hides one trial output; the complete trials cover four target inputs and null. Auditors separately judge target recovery and functional action dependence as supported, contradicted or insufficiently evidenced. Reference labels reflect what visible records establish, rather than hidden mechanism labels. Matched cases can share visible evidence despite different underlying mechanisms. Scripted controls and same-case baselines validate and contextualize performance. Missing replies remain in accounting, and uncertainty is grouped by the 20 matched blocks. This evaluates supplied-record judgments, not discovery of unknown channels or real-world collusion.

**Scope.** The two questions do not establish intent, legal agreement, or that removing the actual message would necessarily change the action. Mechanism names in this overview describe generation; they are not case labels supplied to the auditor. Baseline/metric boxes describe the analysis design, not a claim that all final exports are complete.
